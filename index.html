Advancing Medical Artificial Intelligence

The MAI (Medical AI) lab specialises in different aspects of methodological development and applications of fundamental AI methodologies in medicine and health. Our research involves various aspects of knowledge representation and reasoning, deep learning, time-series analytics and the development of the next generation LLMs in the context of medicine and health. We are based at the Department of Biostatistics and Health Informatics, King's College London and are part of the large PHI Data Lab. Explore Our Research
